SETUP
1. Save the Excel file to your OneDrive and turn on AutoSave.
2. Click any cell inside the table on the Haul_Data sheet.
3. Open Copilot from the Home tab. Paste one prompt at a time.

Tip: answers can differ a little each time you run a prompt.
Always check the key numbers before you trust them.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 - UNDERSTAND THE DATA
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I am a consultant reviewing haul truck performance for a coal mining client.
Explain what this dataset covers: the period, pits, trucks, shifts and what
one row represents. Then suggest the 5 most valuable business questions this
data can answer for the mine manager.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 - CLEAN THE DATA
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2a. Data quality check
Act as a data quality analyst. Check this table for data quality issues:
inconsistent spelling in Shift and Pit, mixed Indonesian and English in
Delay_Reason, blank cells, duplicate rows, and values that look like they
are in the wrong unit. Answer as a table with these columns:
Column | Issue | Example | Rows affected | Suggested fix.

2b. One language for delay reasons
Add a column called Delay_Category that groups Delay_Reason into these
English categories: Rain, Waiting for loader, Breakdown, Tyre, Road
maintenance, Refuelling, None. Treat Hujan and Hujan deras as Rain; Antri
loader and Tunggu excavator as Waiting for loader; any reason containing
Rusak, Mesin rusak or Breakdown as Breakdown; Ban bocor as Tyre; Perbaikan
jalan as Road maintenance; Isi BBM as Refuelling; blank as None.

2c. Calculated columns
Add two calculated columns: Actual_Tonnes = Trips x Avg_Payload_t, and
Fuel_per_Tonne = Fuel_Used_L / Actual_Tonnes. If Avg_Payload_t is greater
than 1000, divide it by 1000 first because it was recorded in kg.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 - ANALYSE (a full C.O.R.E prompt)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Context: We are a consulting team reviewing why haul truck production fell
below target at a coal mine between July and September 2026.
Objective: Identify the top 3 drivers of the production gap, using
Actual_Tonnes against Target_Tonnes, by month, by pit and by
Delay_Category.
Role: Act as a senior mining operations analyst.
Expectation: Respond in a table: Driver | Evidence (with numbers) | Impact
on tonnes | Recommended action. End with a one-sentence executive summary.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 - VISUALISE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Create a line chart of weekly average Fuel_per_Tonne for truck HT-07
compared with the average of all other trucks, from July to September
2026. Then tell me whether HT-07 showed any warning sign before its
breakdown in mid-September.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHALLENGES - what else is hidden in the data?
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Highlight the rows where Avg_Payload_t is above 90.7 tonnes, the rated
payload of the CAT 777E. Which truck overloads most often, and is it one
operator or several? What does that suggest about the root cause?

Rank operators by average Avg_Cycle_Time_min within each pit. Who is the
best operator in each pit, and is anyone on the night shift performing
better than the day shift?

Create a PivotTable of total Downtime_Hours by Pit and Delay_Category on a
new sheet, and add a stacked bar chart.

Now write your own prompt with C.O.R.E:
Context - Objective - Role - Expectation.
