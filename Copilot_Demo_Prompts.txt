SETUP
[ ] Simpan file ke OneDrive / SharePoint. Copilot butuh AutoSave ON.
[ ] Data sudah dalam Excel Table bernama "HaulData". Jangan diubah.
[ ] Buka sheet Haul_Data, klik satu sel di dalam tabel.
[ ] Buka panel Copilot (tab Home > Copilot). Pastikan sudah login akun
    kantor.
[ ] Zoom Excel 120%+, perbesar panel Copilot jika diperlukan.
[ ] Siapkan SALINAN CADANGAN file yang bersih jika diperlukan (prompt yang menambah
    kolom akan mengubah file).
[ ] Buka file TXT ini di layar kedua untuk copy-paste prompt.

Tip: answers can differ a little each time you run a prompt.
Always check the key numbers before you trust them.

===P1:
Hi Copilot, act as my mining analyst. 
Let's understand this haul truck data first, then follow me to analyze it: 
why is production below target? Put your work on a new sheet called Analysis.

===P2:
Some entries look inconsistent: spelling in Shift and Pit, Indonesian and English in Delay_Reason, 
payload in kg instead of tonnes, and duplicate rows. 
Clean them, list what you fixed, and redo the analysis on the same sheet.

===P3:
Prompt 3
Step 3 of 3: visualize only. Add charts for target achievement by month and by pit, and downtime hours by reason, 
plus a 3-line executive summary on the Analysis sheet. Then stop.
